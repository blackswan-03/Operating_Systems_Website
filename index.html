Welcome to the operating systems website!

Have you ever wondered what that logo in the corner of your desktop represents? Or what makes the mouse and windows appear on your screen? It's called an OPERATING SYSTEM,, and it's the software you interact with every time you use your computer or smartphone. It makes the applications on your computer communicate with the hardware. Every computer has an operating system, whether it's obvious or not. Some operating systems have extensive graphical interfaces, while others seem like little more than a text command-line.

Use the links at the top of the screen to learn more about some of the most popular operating systems out there: Microsoft's "Windows", Apple's "Mac OS", and a community-run family of operating systems called "Linux". Afterwards, you can have some fun in the "Extras" section. If you want to get even more information on this fascinating topic, check out the "Sources" link, where all of the information in this website came from!
